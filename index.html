Caponiac is the stage name of Anthony Jordan, a US gangsta rapper from Lompoc, California, known for his Central Coast hip-hop. Releasing music since the late 90s, with popular tracks like "We Could Be Friends (Remix)" and albums like Bacc N da Game, and has been active on platforms like Apple Music, Spotify, and Discogs.
